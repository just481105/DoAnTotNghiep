> Cụm phân từ (participle phrase) gồm một **phân từ** (V-ing hoặc V3/ed) cùng tân ngữ, bổ ngữ hoặc trạng ngữ đi kèm. Dùng để **rút gọn** câu và tránh lặp lại.

## I. Mệnh đề phân từ và cụm phân từ

### 1. Ba loại phân từ

| Loại | Dạng | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hiện tại | **V-ing** | chủ động, đồng thời hoặc cùng chủ thể hành động | **Walking** along the beach, he found a conch. |
| Quá khứ | **V3/ed** | bị động | **Exhausted** from the long journey, he went straight to bed. |
| Hoàn thành | **Having + V3/ed** | chủ động, xảy ra **trước** hành động chính | **Having graduated** in June, she began looking for a job. |

Dạng phủ định và bị động của hoàn thành:

- Phủ định: **Not + V-ing** / **Not having + V3**: *Not knowing the way, they asked a policeman.*
- Bị động: **(Being) + V3** / **Having been + V3**: *Having been warned about the storm, the fishermen stayed ashore.*

### 2. Vị trí của cụm phân từ

| Vị trí | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| Đầu câu (+ dấu phẩy) | **Smiling happily**, she greeted the couple. |
| Giữa câu (đặt giữa hai dấu phẩy) | The vase, **broken into several pieces**, cannot be repaired. |
| Cuối câu | I've just seen Tom **playing football in the schoolyard**. |

### 3. Rút gọn mệnh đề quan hệ

Chỉ rút gọn được khi đại từ quan hệ (*who, which, that*) làm **chủ ngữ**.

| Trường hợp | Cách rút gọn | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| Chủ động | **V-ing** (bỏ đại từ quan hệ và *be*) | The man **who is standing** at the gate → The man **standing** at the gate |
| Bị động | **V3/ed** | The book **which was written** by him → The book **written** by him |
| Sau *the first, the last, the only, the second, so sánh nhất* | **to V** | She was the first person **to know** the answer. |
| Chỉ mục đích hoặc sự cần làm | **to V** | I have a lot of homework **to do**. |

### 4. Rút gọn mệnh đề trạng ngữ (cùng chủ ngữ)

Hai mệnh đề **phải cùng chủ ngữ**. Chủ ngữ của mệnh đề phụ được bỏ, động từ đổi thành phân từ.

| Quan hệ | Câu đầy đủ | Rút gọn |
|---|---|---|
| Đồng thời / cách thức | While she was walking home, she met Tom. | **Walking** home, she met Tom. |
| Nguyên nhân (chủ động) | Because he heard the news, he burst into tears. | **Hearing** the news, he burst into tears. |
| Nguyên nhân (bị động) | Because he was exhausted, he went to bed. | **Exhausted** from the trip, he went to bed. |
| Trước - sau (V xong trước) | After he had finished his homework, he went out. | **Having finished** his homework, he went out. |
| Phủ định | Because she didn't have a car, she took the bus. | **Not having** a car, she took the bus. |

> Có thể giữ liên từ: ***After** finishing, **Before** leaving, **While** walking, **When** crossing, **On** arriving*.

### 5. Lỗi thường gặp

| Lỗi | Câu sai | Câu đúng |
|---|---|---|
| **Khác chủ ngữ** (dangling participle) | *Running to catch the bus, the driver honked the horn.* | Running to catch the bus, **I heard** the driver honk the horn. |
| **Sai thì** (hành động xảy ra trước nhưng dùng V-ing) | *Reading the book, he recommended it to his friends.* | **Having read** the book, he recommended it to his friends. |
| **Sai chủ động / bị động** | *Having been finished his work, he went home.* | **Having finished** his work, he went home. |
| **Sai dạng phủ định** | *Having not known the answer, ...* | **Not having known / Not knowing** the answer, ... |

**Mẹo kiểm tra:** thử đặt chủ ngữ của mệnh đề chính trước phân từ. Nếu chủ ngữ thực hiện hành động đó thì dùng V-ing / having V3; nếu chủ ngữ **chịu tác động** thì dùng V3/ed.

### 6. Chức năng của cụm phân từ

1. **Bổ nghĩa cho danh từ** (rút gọn mệnh đề quan hệ): *The car **restored by Tom** is a classic model.*
2. **Thay mệnh đề trạng ngữ:** *Having finished his homework, John went to bed.* (thay *After he had finished his homework*)
3. **Nối các hành động liên tiếp:** *Running quickly, swimming across the river and climbing over the wall, the inmate escaped.*

## II. Ví dụ

1. **V-ing (chủ động):** *Walking along the beach, he found a conch.*
2. **V3 (bị động):** *Exhausted from the long journey, he went straight to bed.*
3. **Having V3 (xảy ra trước):** *Having graduated in June, she began looking for a job.*
4. **Phủ định:** *Not knowing the way, they asked a policeman.*
5. **Rút gọn mệnh đề quan hệ chủ động:** *The girl sitting next to me is my cousin.* (= who is sitting)
6. **Rút gọn mệnh đề quan hệ bị động:** *The book placed on the top shelf belongs to my sister.* (= which is placed)
7. **to V sau số thứ tự:** *She was the first student to finish the test.*
8. **Giữa câu:** *The woman, dressed in a white dress, entered the room confidently.*
9. **Lỗi khác chủ ngữ:** *Running to catch the bus, the driver honked the horn.* → *Running to catch the bus, I heard the driver honk the horn.*
10. **Lỗi sai thì:** *Reading the book, he recommended it to his friends.* → *Having read the book, he recommended it to his friends.*

## III. Bài tập nhanh

**Bài 1.** Nối hai câu thành một câu, dùng cụm phân từ.

1. She opened the door. She saw a package on the floor.
2. He had finished his homework. He went out to play.
3. The house was built in 1990. It still looks new.
4. She was tired after a long day. She went to bed early.
5. They did not know the way. They asked a policeman.
6. The girl is sitting next to me. She is my cousin.

**Bài 2.** Chia động từ trong ngoặc.

1. ____ (walk) along the river, we saw many birds.
2. ____ (finish) the exam, the students left the room.
3. The letters ____ (write) by my grandfather are very old.
4. ____ (not / know) what to say, he kept silent.
5. ____ (warn) about the storm, the fishermen stayed ashore.

**Bài 3.** Chọn đáp án đúng.

1. ____ the news, she burst into tears.
   A. Hear  B. Hearing  C. Heard  D. To hear
2. ____ his homework, he went out to play.
   A. Finishing  B. Finished  C. Having finished  D. To finish
3. The man ____ at the gate is our new teacher.
   A. stands  B. standing  C. stood  D. to stand
4. The photos ____ by my father were beautiful.
   A. taking  B. take  C. taken  D. to take
5. She was the first person ____ the answer.
   A. knowing  B. known  C. to know  D. know
6. ____ a car, he has to go to work by bus.
   A. Not having  B. Having not  C. Not to have  D. No having
7. ____ by the noise, the baby began to cry.
   A. Frighten  B. Frightening  C. Frightened  D. To frighten

**Bài 4.** Tìm và sửa lỗi sai.

1. Walking along the street, a car almost hit me.
2. Having been finished his work, he went home.
3. Not knowing the answer, the question was left blank.
4. The boy standing by the door are my brother.

**Bài 1**

1. Opening the door, she saw a package on the floor.
2. Having finished his homework, he went out to play.
3. Built in 1990, the house still looks new.
4. Tired after a long day, she went to bed early. (hoặc: *Being tired after a long day, ...*)
5. Not knowing the way, they asked a policeman.
6. The girl sitting next to me is my cousin.

**Bài 2**

1. **Walking**
2. **Having finished**
3. **written** (rút gọn bị động: *which were written*)
4. **Not knowing**
5. **Warned** / **Having been warned** (bị động)

**Bài 3**

1. **B**: *Hearing the news, she burst into tears.*
2. **C**: hoàn thành xong trước rồi mới đi chơi.
3. **B**: rút gọn chủ động, *who is standing*.
4. **C**: rút gọn bị động, *which were taken*.
5. **C**: *to V* sau *the first*.
6. **A**: phủ định là *Not having ...*
7. **C**: *Frightened by the noise*: bị động, em bé bị làm cho sợ.

**Bài 4**

1. Sai khác chủ ngữ (*a car* không thể *walk*). Sửa: Walking along the street, **I was almost hit by a car.**
2. Sai dạng. Sửa: **Having finished** his work, he went home.
3. Sai khác chủ ngữ (*the question* không biết câu trả lời). Sửa: Not knowing the answer, **I left the question blank.**
4. Chủ ngữ là *the boy* (số ít). Sửa: The boy standing by the door **is** my brother.
