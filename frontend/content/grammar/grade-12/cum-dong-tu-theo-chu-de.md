> Học cụm động từ (phrasal verb) theo chủ đề sẽ dễ nhớ hơn học theo bảng chữ cái.

## I. Cụm động từ theo chủ đề

**Lưu ý chung:**

- Cụm động từ = động từ + giới từ/trạng từ, nghĩa thường **khác** nghĩa của từng từ riêng lẻ.
- Cụm **tách được** (separable): tân ngữ có thể đứng giữa. *Turn the light off.* Nếu tân ngữ là đại từ thì **bắt buộc** tách: *Turn **it** off* (không viết *turn off it*).
- Cụm **không tách được** (inseparable): *look after, look for, get over, run into, come across*. *She looks after **him**.*
- Sau *look forward to*, *take to*, *give up*, *keep on* thường là **V-ing**: *look forward to seeing*, *give up smoking*.

## II. Ví dụ

1. **Chọn đúng nghĩa của cụm:** *The meeting was **called off** because of the storm.* → *called off* = bị hủy.
2. **Tân ngữ là đại từ phải tách:** *The music is too loud. Please turn **it** down.* (không viết *turn down it*)
3. **Theo sau là V-ing:** *I'm looking forward to **seeing** you.* / *He gave up **smoking**.*
4. **Cụm không tách được:** *She is looking after **her grandmother**.* / *She is looking after **her**.*
5. **Một cụm, nhiều nghĩa:** *take off* = cất cánh (máy bay) / cởi (quần áo); *turn down* = vặn nhỏ / từ chối.
6. **Đề thi THPT Quốc gia:**
   *Don't put ____ until tomorrow what you can do today.* → **off** (put off: trì hoãn)
   *She has not yet got ____ her recent illness.* → **over** (get over: hồi phục)

## III. Bài tập nhanh

**Bài 1.** Điền cụm động từ thích hợp (chia đúng dạng): *take off, run into, call off, get over, turn down, run out of, bring up, fill in, look forward to, put off*

1. Please ____ your shoes before entering the room.
2. I ____ my old teacher in the supermarket yesterday.
3. The match was ____ because of the heavy rain.
4. She has quickly ____ her illness.
5. Can you ____ the volume? It's too loud.
6. We've ____ petrol, so we need to find a gas station.
7. He was ____ by his grandparents.
8. Please ____ this form before submitting it.
9. I'm ____ seeing you next week.
10. Don't ____ until tomorrow what you can do today.

**Bài 2.** Chọn đáp án đúng.

1. My family has come ____ flu. You should take care of yourself.
   A. down with  B. through  C. over  D. up
2. It's too noisy. Do you mind if I ____ the TV?
   A. turn on  B. turn up  C. turn in  D. turn off
3. He asked me to ____ his cat while he was away.
   A. get away  B. take care of  C. take after  D. care
4. Our trip was ____ because my father was ill.
   A. cut off  B. put on  C. called off  D. called on
5. Natural resources are being used ____. We should use renewable energy.
   A. at  B. on  C. in  D. up

**Bài 3.** Tìm và sửa lỗi sai.

1. I'm looking forward to see you soon.
2. He gave up to smoke last year.
3. Please turn off it before you leave.
4. The meeting was put on until next week because of the storm.
