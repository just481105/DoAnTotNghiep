> Mệnh đề trạng ngữ (adverbial clause) là mệnh đề **phụ thuộc**, có chủ ngữ và động từ, đóng vai trò **trạng ngữ** (chỉ thời gian, nguyên nhân, mục đích, nhượng bộ ...). Nó **không đứng một mình** thành câu.

## I. Mệnh đề trạng ngữ: thời gian, mục đích, nhượng bộ (tổng hợp)

### 1. Tổng quan các loại mệnh đề trạng ngữ

| Loại | Liên từ thường gặp | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **Thời gian** | when, while, as, before, after, since, until, as soon as, once, by the time | **When** I arrived, it was raining. |
| **Nguyên nhân** | because, since, as, now that | **Because** I'm sick, I can't come. |
| **Mục đích** | so that, in order that, (lest) | He studies hard **so that** he can pass. |
| **Kết quả** | so ... that, such ... that | It was **so** hot **that** I stayed in. |
| **Nhượng bộ** | although, though, even though, while, whereas, even if | **Although** it rained, we went out. |
| **Điều kiện** | if, unless, as long as, provided that | **If** it rains, I'll stay home. |
| **So sánh** | as ... as, than, as if, as though | He is **as** good at study **as** at games. |
| **Cách thức** | as, as if, as though, (the) way | He did it **the way** I expected. |
| **Nơi chốn** | where, wherever, anywhere | They went **where** no one could find them. |

> *Unless* là liên từ **điều kiện** (không phải nguyên nhân), *during* là **giới từ** (không phải liên từ).

### 2. Vị trí của mệnh đề trạng ngữ

- **Đầu câu** (có dấu phẩy sau mệnh đề): *Before he got home, he was robbed.*
- **Cuối câu** (thường không có dấu phẩy): *He was robbed before he got home.*
- Mệnh đề trạng ngữ bổ nghĩa cho **tính từ hoặc trạng từ** thì đứng **ngay sau** từ đó: *The weather is so hot **that I can't go out**.* / *The deal turned out better **than I expected**.*

**Mệnh đề trạng ngữ và cụm trạng ngữ:** mệnh đề có **chủ ngữ + động từ**, cụm trạng ngữ thì không.

- Mệnh đề: *She ate the chocolate **faster than I could**.*
- Cụm: *She ate the chocolate **with gusto**.*

### 3. Mệnh đề trạng ngữ chỉ thời gian

| Liên từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| when | khi | **When** I was young, I lived in Hue. |
| while / as | trong khi | **While** I was walking, I saw her. |
| just as | đúng lúc | He came **just as** the party was about to start. |
| before | trước khi | **Before** he got home, he was robbed. |
| after | sau khi | **After** she had finished, she left. |
| since | kể từ khi | I haven't met my mom **since** I came to Hanoi. |
| until / till | cho đến khi | Wait here **until** I come back. |
| as soon as / once | ngay khi | **As soon as** I arrive, I'll call you. |
| by the time | vào lúc | **By the time** we arrived, the film had started. |

**Lưu ý về thì:**

- **Không dùng *will* trong mệnh đề thời gian** nói về tương lai: *I'll call you when I **arrive**.* (không viết ~~when I will arrive~~)
- *since*: mệnh đề chính ở **hiện tại hoàn thành**, mệnh đề *since* ở **quá khứ đơn**.
- *by the time* + hiện tại đơn → mệnh đề chính **will have + V3**; *by the time* + quá khứ đơn → **had + V3**.

### 4. Mệnh đề trạng ngữ chỉ mục đích

Mục đích trả lời câu hỏi **"để làm gì?"** (khác nguyên nhân là "tại sao đã xảy ra?").

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| **in order to / so as to + V** (cùng chủ ngữ) | She has been studying non-stop **in order to** pass the exam. |
| **so as not to / in order not to + V** (phủ định) | He left early **so as not to** miss the bus. |
| **so that / in order that + S + can/could/will/would + V** (khác chủ ngữ) | I lent him my motorbike **so that** he could get there on time. |
| **lest + S + (should) + V** (trang trọng) | He spoke quietly **lest** he should wake the baby. |

> *so that* + mệnh đề chỉ **mục đích**, khác với *so ... that* chỉ **kết quả**. *Because, since, as* chỉ nguyên nhân, **không** chỉ mục đích.

### 5. Mệnh đề trạng ngữ chỉ nhượng bộ

Thừa nhận một điều trái ngược với ý ở mệnh đề chính ("mặc dù ... nhưng ...").

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| **although / though / even though + S + V** | **Although** he was tired, he kept working. |
| **despite / in spite of + N / V-ing** (cụm, không phải mệnh đề) | **Despite** the rain, we went out. / **Despite** being tired, he kept working. |
| **despite / in spite of the fact that + S + V** | **In spite of the fact that** he was tired, he kept working. |
| **while / whereas + S + V** (đối lập) | **Whereas** I like tea, she prefers coffee. |
| **Adj / Adv + as + S + V** (nhấn mạnh) | **Tired as** he was, he kept working. |
| **no matter + wh- / whatever / however + S + V** | **However** hard he tried, he failed. |

> **Không** dùng *although* cùng với *but* trong cùng một câu: ~~Although he was tired, but he kept working~~.

### 6. Rút gọn mệnh đề trạng ngữ (cùng chủ ngữ)

Chỉ rút gọn khi hai mệnh đề có **cùng chủ ngữ**.

| Trường hợp | Cách rút gọn | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **Chủ động** | Bỏ chủ ngữ, động từ → **V-ing**; có thể giữ hoặc bỏ liên từ | While I was walking down the street, I saw her. → **While walking** / **Walking** down the street, I saw her. |
| **Bị động** | Bỏ chủ ngữ và *be* → **V3/ed** (hoặc **Being V3**) | Because I was promoted, I have a higher salary. → **Promoted**, / **Being promoted**, I have a higher salary. |
| **Động từ *be* / nối + adj / N** | Bỏ chủ ngữ và *be*, giữ tính từ / danh từ | Although they are in a hurry, ... → **Although in a hurry**, ... / When I am busy → **When busy**, ... |
| **Nguyên nhân** | Bỏ liên từ và chủ ngữ, dùng V-ing / Being | Because I'm sick, ... → **Being sick**, ... |
| **Hành động xảy ra trước** | **Having + V3/ed** | After she had cleaned the house, she watched TV. → **Having cleaned** the house, she watched TV. |
| **Phủ định** | **Not + V-ing / Not having + V3** | Because he didn't know the answer, ... → **Not knowing** the answer, ... |

> Liên từ có thể giữ lại sau khi rút gọn: *when, while, before, after, since, once, until, although, though*. Liên từ chỉ nguyên nhân (*because, since, as*) thì **bỏ** khi rút gọn.

## II. Ví dụ

1. **Thời gian:** *He came just as the party was about to start.*
2. **Thời gian (không dùng will):** *I'll call you as soon as I arrive.*
3. **Thời gian (since):** *I have not met my mom since I came to Hanoi.*
4. **Mục đích (cùng chủ ngữ):** *She has been studying non-stop in order to pass the exam.*
5. **Mục đích (khác chủ ngữ):** *I lent him my motorbike so that he could get to the meeting on time.*
6. **Nhượng bộ:** *Although they did the work in a short time, we weren't in a hurry.*
7. **despite + V-ing:** *Despite having the best intentions, I couldn't help my daughter.*
8. **Rút gọn chủ động:** *While I was walking down the street, I saw her.* → *Walking down the street, I saw her.*
9. **Rút gọn bị động:** *When it is heated, ice turns into water.* → *When heated, ice turns into water.*
10. **Having + V3:** *Having passed the exam, Jane was given a present.*

## III. Bài tập nhanh

**Bài 1.** Chọn đáp án đúng.

1. ____ he was tired, he kept working.
   A. Because  B. Although  C. So that  D. Unless
2. She studies hard ____ she can win a scholarship.
   A. so that  B. although  C. because of  D. unless
3. ____ the rain, we went for a walk.
   A. Although  B. Despite  C. Because  D. While
4. I'll call you as soon as I ____ home.
   A. will arrive  B. arrive  C. arrived  D. am arriving
5. By the time we arrived, the film ____.
   A. already started  B. has already started  C. had already started  D. will start
6. He left early ____ miss the last bus.
   A. in order to  B. so as not to  C. so that  D. lest not
7. ____ as he is, he is not happy.
   A. Rich  B. Richly  C. Wealth  D. Riches
8. We have been friends ____ we were children.
   A. since  B. while  C. until  D. during
9. ____ his poor English, he got the job.
   A. Although  B. In spite of  C. Even though  D. Despite of
10. Don't leave ____ I come back.
    A. until  B. while  C. since  D. after

**Bài 2.** Rút gọn mệnh đề trạng ngữ.

1. While she was waiting for the bus, she read a book.
2. After he had finished his homework, he played games.
3. Because she was tired, she went to bed early.
4. Although he was injured, he continued to play.
5. Because he didn't know the answer, he stayed silent.
6. When it is heated, ice turns into water.

**Bài 3.** Tìm và sửa lỗi sai.

1. Although he was tired, but he kept working.
2. Despite he was tired, he kept working.
3. I'll call you when I will arrive.
4. She studied hard so as she could pass the exam.
5. As being promoted, I have a higher salary.
6. Having cleaned the house, she watch TV and relax.

<summary>Đáp án</summary>

**Bài 1**

1. **B**: nhượng bộ ("mặc dù mệt vẫn làm").
2. **A**: mục đích, *so that* + S + can.
3. **B**: *Despite* + danh từ (*the rain*).
4. **B**: mệnh đề thời gian tương lai dùng hiện tại đơn.
5. **C**: hành động xong trước một mốc quá khứ → quá khứ hoàn thành.
6. **B**: mục đích phủ định, cùng chủ ngữ → *so as not to + V*.
7. **A**: *Adj + as + S + V* (*Rich as he is*).
8. **A**: *since* + mốc thời gian, mệnh đề chính hiện tại hoàn thành.
9. **B**: *In spite of* + cụm danh từ. *Despite of* là sai.
10. **A**: *until* (cho đến khi).

**Bài 2**

1. While waiting for the bus, she read a book. (hoặc: *Waiting for the bus, ...*)
2. Having finished his homework, he played games.
3. Being tired / Tired, she went to bed early.
4. Although injured, he continued to play.
5. Not knowing the answer, he stayed silent.
6. When heated, ice turns into water.

**Bài 3**

1. Bỏ *but*: Although he was tired, he kept working.
2. *Despite* không đi với mệnh đề. Sửa: **Although he was tired**, ... hoặc **Despite being tired**, ...
3. Mệnh đề thời gian tương lai không dùng *will*. Sửa: I'll call you when I **arrive**.
4. *so as* đi với *to V*. Sửa: She studied hard **so as to pass** the exam. (hoặc: **so that she could pass**)
5. Sai khi giữ liên từ *As* với *being*. Sửa: **Being promoted**, I have a higher salary. (hoặc: **Promoted**, ... / **Since I was promoted**, ...)
6. Chia sai thì và thiếu song song: *Having cleaned the house, she **watches** TV and **relaxes**.*
